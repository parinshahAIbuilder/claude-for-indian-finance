# Disclosure battery

Test for presence, then test that what is present is substantive. A topic named
in the accounting policy note is not the disclosure.

Where an item is absent, the audit evidence often already exists in the CARO
annexure. The auditor establishes the fact and the financial statements omit it.
That pairing is a strong finding: cite the CARO clause.

## C. Ind AS by standard

**Ind AS 115 Revenue.** Disaggregation by category and by timing of transfer;
contract balances including contract assets, contract liabilities and revenue
recognised from opening contract liabilities; remaining performance obligations;
reconciliation of contracted price to revenue recognised. A product line split
alone is not disaggregation.

**Ind AS 116 Leases.** As lessee: right of use assets by class, lease
liabilities, maturity analysis, amounts in profit or loss, total cash outflow,
short term and low value lease expense. **As lessor:** operating lease income,
maturity analysis of lease payments receivable by year band, and the risk
management strategy for rights retained. For a company that sub licenses space,
an absent lessor section is a material omission, and it is the most commonly
missed disclosure in this sector.

**Ind AS 12 Income taxes.** Reconciliation of tax expense to accounting profit at
the applicable rate, para 81(c). The applicable rate and any change in it, para
81(d). Unused tax losses and credits with expiry. Evidence supporting a deferred
tax asset where there is a history of losses, para 82. MAT credit entitlement
where section 115JB applies.

**Ind AS 7.** Reconciliation of liabilities arising from financing activities,
para 44A. Non cash transactions. Restricted cash.

**Ind AS 8.** Where an amount is charged directly to reserves and described as a
prior period item: nature of the error, the correction to each line item, **the
effect on basic and diluted EPS**, restated comparatives, and a third balance
sheet.

**Ind AS 24.** Key management personnel compensation split into short term
benefits, post employment benefits, other long term benefits, termination
benefits and share based payment. Aggregate remuneration is not sufficient.
Interest paid on borrowings from KMP is a transaction requiring disclosure.

**Ind AS 107.** Split of borrowings between fixed and floating rate. A
sensitivity stated to cover the floating portion while applying the shift to
total borrowings is internally inconsistent. Maturity analyses on an
undiscounted basis where so described.

**Ind AS 32.** Redeemable preference shares are a financial liability, not
equity. Where they sit inside equity share capital on the face, raise it.

**Ind AS 36 and 28.** Impairment indicators for investments in and loans to
associates, particularly where the associate's own auditor has reported cash
losses or the associate carries negative reserves.

**Ind AS 108.** Entity wide disclosures: revenue from external customers by
geography, non current assets by geography, and major customer concentration,
even for a single segment entity. Blank tables are a presentation failure.

## E. Companies Act

- Section 186(4): particulars of loans given, investments made, guarantees and
  security provided, **and the purpose** for which each is to be used.
- Section 135: CSR. The applicability test is net worth, turnover or net profit in
  the **immediately preceding** financial year. The three year average is the
  spend computation, not the trigger.
- Section 134(3)(l): material changes between the year end and the date of the
  Board's report.
- Section 197(12) and Rule 5: remuneration ratios, in the Board's report.
- Section 188 and AOC 2: related party contracts.
- Section 129(3) and AOC 1: salient features of subsidiaries and associates.

## F. SEBI

**ICDR.** Where the issue exceeded one hundred crore rupees, a monitoring agency
was required under Regulation 41, reporting quarterly until full utilisation.
Check the annual report mentions it. Promoter shares under lock in may be pledged
only in the circumstances set out in Regulation 21, and for minimum promoters'
contribution only where the loan finances an object of the issue.

**LODR.** Regulation 32, statement of deviation in use of proceeds and where
unutilised amounts are parked. Regulation 23, related party transaction approvals
and the materiality threshold. Regulation 17(1)(b), board composition, at least
half independent where the chairperson is an executive promoter. Regulation 24A,
secretarial compliance report. Regulation 34 and Schedule V, annual report
contents. Chapter V where non convertible securities are listed.

**SAST.** Regulation 28(3) defines encumbrance to include pledge, lien and non
disposal undertaking. A statement that shares are encumbered but not pledged is
at best misleading. Regulation 31 requires disclosure within seven working days.

## G. Income tax

- Section 115BAA election is incompatible with MAT under section 115JB. Where the
  current tax charge implies MAT while the deferred tax working uses the 115BAA
  rate, the rate basis is unresolved.
- Section 43B(h): amounts payable to micro and small enterprises not paid within
  the time limit are disallowed. A large disallowance against the MSME payable
  balance is worth disclosing alongside the MSMED note.
- Section 92E and Form 3CEB where there are international transactions with
  associated enterprises. Foreign revenue in the geographic segment note is the
  trigger to ask.
- Carried forward losses utilised, and the resulting Ind AS 12 disclosure.
