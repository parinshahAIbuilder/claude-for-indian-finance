# 04 — Notes to accounts and accounting policies

## Note numbering rule
Note 1 = Corporate information. Note 2 = Basis of preparation, material accounting policies,
critical estimates and judgements. Notes 3 onward follow face-statement order: balance sheet top
to bottom, then P&L top to bottom, then other disclosures. Each face line shows its note number;
each note's closing total equals the face line by formula (`=Notes!X` on the face, and a check
cell `=Notes!X - BS!Y` that must be 0).

## Accounting policies — write as material policies, specific to the entity

Under Ind AS (amended Ind AS 1) and IFRS, disclose *material* accounting policy information, not
boilerplate. For each, state the entity's actual choice:

- Basis of preparation: compliance statement, historical cost except items at fair value,
  functional/presentation currency, rounding, current/non-current classification (operating cycle)
- Revenue recognition — performance obligations, timing (point/over time), variable consideration
- PPE — cost model/revaluation; depreciation method; useful lives (and whether they differ from
  Schedule II, with justification); residual value; component accounting
- Intangibles — useful lives, amortisation, impairment
- Leases — lessee: ROU and lease liability, discount rate (IBR), short-term/low-value exemption;
  lessor: operating/finance classification
- Financial instruments — classification (amortised cost / FVOCI / FVTPL), ECL approach
  (simplified matrix for receivables), derecognition, compound instruments (CCPS/OCD split)
- Inventories — cost formula (FIFO/weighted average), NRV
- Employee benefits — defined contribution, defined benefit (projected unit credit), OCI for
  remeasurements, compensated absences, share-based payments
- Income taxes — current, deferred (balance sheet approach), MAT/new regime choice
- Foreign currency — transaction and translation
- Provisions, contingent liabilities and contingent assets
- Borrowing costs — capitalisation on qualifying assets
- Impairment of non-financial assets
- Cash and cash equivalents definition
- Segment reporting basis (CODM)
- EPS
- Government grants (if any)
- Business combinations and goodwill (consolidated)

## Critical estimates and judgements note
List only the real ones: useful lives, ECL, defined benefit assumptions, lease term and IBR,
deferred tax asset recoverability, fair value of unquoted instruments, revenue (principal vs
agent, variable consideration), provisions and contingencies, impairment.

## Language rules
- Present tense, third person ("The Company recognises…").
- No hedging adjectives. No marketing words.
- Every amount in notes uses the same rounding unit as the face statements; state "(All amounts
  in ₹ lakhs unless otherwise stated)" at the top of each page.
