# 08 — Comparison, ratios and executive summary

## Prior-period comparison (Comparison tab)
For every face line and note sub-line: current, previous, change (amount), change (%), flag.
Flags: ≥10% = review; ≥25% = commentary required. Commentary is factual (driver + number), e.g.
"Finance costs up 38% due to ₹150 crore NCD issued in Q2; full-year interest impact ₹X crore."
If the prior period was regrouped, show "Previous year figures regrouped/reclassified" with a
reconciliation of the changes.

Optional: three- or five-year trend if earlier audited data is provided.

## Schedule III mandatory ratios (Div II/Div I) — show both years, % change, and reason if >25%

| Ratio | Numerator | Denominator |
|---|---|---|
| Current ratio | Current assets | Current liabilities |
| Debt-equity ratio | Total debt (borrowings + lease liabilities, state policy) | Shareholders' equity |
| Debt service coverage ratio | Earnings available for debt service (PAT + non-cash operating expenses + interest + other adjustments) | Debt service (interest + lease payments + principal repayments) |
| Return on equity | Net profit after tax − preference dividend | Average shareholders' equity |
| Inventory turnover | COGS or sales | Average inventory |
| Trade receivables turnover | Net credit sales | Average trade receivables |
| Trade payables turnover | Net credit purchases | Average trade payables |
| Net capital turnover | Net sales | Working capital (CA − CL) |
| Net profit ratio | Net profit | Net sales |
| Return on capital employed | EBIT | Capital employed (tangible net worth + total debt + DTL) |
| Return on investment | Income from investments | Average investments (time-weighted) |

Define every numerator/denominator in a footnote — definitions vary and auditors check consistency
year to year.

## Management / investor metrics (Summary tab)
Revenue growth; EBITDA and EBITDA margin (show pre- and post-Ind AS 116 for lease-heavy sectors);
PAT margin; EPS; book value per share; net debt and net debt/EBITDA; interest coverage; cash
conversion (CFO/EBITDA); working capital days (debtor, inventory, creditor, cash conversion
cycle); capex and capex/revenue; free cash flow; ROCE/ROE trend; effective tax rate.
For listed companies add market data if provided or searchable: market cap, P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA,
dividend payout.

## Executive summary (one page, board audience)
Structure:
1. Headline — three numbers that matter (revenue, EBITDA/PAT, net debt or cash) with YoY change.
2. Performance — two or three sentences on drivers.
3. Balance sheet and liquidity — leverage, working capital, cash.
4. Key judgements and audit-sensitive items — top 3 from Open Points / critical estimates.
5. Compliance flags — RPT approvals pending, MSME overdue, covenant ratios near thresholds,
   Schedule III ratio movements needing explanation.
Plain language, no technical depth unless a number needs context. Every figure on this page
links by formula to the statements.

## Charts (in Summary tab or HTML dashboard if requested)
Revenue and EBITDA bar (2 years), cost structure % of revenue, working capital days, net debt
waterfall (opening → CFO → capex → financing → closing).
