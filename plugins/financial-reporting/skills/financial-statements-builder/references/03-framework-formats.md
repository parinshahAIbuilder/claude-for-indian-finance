# 03 — Framework formats

Pick one framework per set. Confirm current-year amendments before use (MCA notifications for
Schedule III and Ind AS; IASB for IFRS; FASB ASUs for US GAAP).

## India — Companies Act 2013, Schedule III

| Division | Applies to | Statements |
|---|---|---|
| Division I | Companies following Accounting Standards (AS / Indian GAAP) | Balance Sheet, Statement of P&L, Cash Flow (AS 3), notes |
| Division II | Ind AS companies other than NBFCs | Balance Sheet, Statement of P&L incl. OCI, SOCE, Cash Flow (Ind AS 7), notes |
| Division III | Ind AS NBFCs | Liquidity-order balance sheet (financial / non-financial), P&L incl. OCI, SOCE, Cash Flow |

### Division II balance sheet skeleton
- **ASSETS** — Non-current assets: (a) PPE, (b) CWIP, (c) Investment property, (d) Goodwill,
  (e) Other intangible assets, (f) Intangible assets under development, (g) Biological assets,
  (h) Financial assets — (i) Investments, (ii) Trade receivables, (iii) Loans, (iv) Others;
  (i) Deferred tax assets (net), (j) Other non-current assets.
  Current assets: (a) Inventories, (b) Financial assets — (i) Investments, (ii) Trade receivables,
  (iii) Cash and cash equivalents, (iv) Bank balances other than (iii), (v) Loans, (vi) Others;
  (c) Current tax assets (net), (d) Other current assets.
- **EQUITY AND LIABILITIES** — Equity: (a) Equity share capital, (b) Other equity.
  Non-current liabilities: Financial liabilities — (i) Borrowings, (ia) Lease liabilities,
  (ii) Trade payables [MSME / others], (iii) Other financial liabilities; Provisions; Deferred tax
  liabilities (net); Other non-current liabilities. Current liabilities: same structure +
  Current tax liabilities (net).
- Right-of-use assets are presented under PPE or as a separate line — be consistent.

### Division II Statement of Profit and Loss
I Revenue from operations; II Other income; III Total income; IV Expenses (cost of materials
consumed, purchases of stock-in-trade, changes in inventories, employee benefits expense, finance
costs, depreciation and amortisation expense, other expenses); V Profit before exceptional items
and tax; VI Exceptional items; VII PBT; VIII Tax expense (current, deferred); IX Profit from
continuing operations; X–XII Discontinued operations; XIII Profit for the period; XIV OCI (items
that will not / will be reclassified, and related tax); XV Total comprehensive income; XVI EPS
(basic, diluted) — continuing, discontinued, total.

### Division I differences (AS)
"Shareholders' funds" not "Equity"; "Reserves and surplus"; "Long-term / Short-term" captions;
"Fixed assets — Tangible / Intangible / CWIP"; no OCI, no SOCE (reserves movement in notes);
"Short-term loans and advances" / "Long-term loans and advances".

### Rounding (Schedule III)
Turnover < ₹100 crore: nearest hundreds, thousands, lakhs or millions (or decimals).
Turnover ≥ ₹100 crore: nearest lakhs, millions or crores (or decimals). One unit throughout.

### Schedule III additional disclosures (2021 amendments — check current notifications)
Shareholding of promoters with % change; trade receivables and payables ageing schedules (with
disputed/undisputed, MSME split for payables); CWIP and intangibles-under-development ageing and
overdue/over-budget completion schedules; title deeds not held in company name; revaluation of
PPE by registered valuer; loans to promoters/directors/KMP/related parties repayable on demand or
without terms; benami property proceedings; borrowings against current assets — quarterly
returns reconciliation with banks; wilful defaulter status; transactions with struck-off
companies; pending charge registration/satisfaction; compliance with number of layers; scheme of
arrangements; utilisation of borrowed funds and share premium (intermediary rules); undisclosed
income surrendered; CSR (amount required, spent, shortfall, reason, related party contributions,
unspent account movement); crypto/virtual currency; the 11 ratios with reasons for >25% change.

## IFRS (IAS 1 presentation)
Statement of financial position (current/non-current or liquidity order); statement of profit or
loss and OCI (single or two statements; by nature or by function — if by function, disclose
nature of expenses); statement of changes in equity; statement of cash flows (IAS 7); notes.
**IFRS 18 "Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements"** replaces IAS 1 for annual
periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027 (early adoption permitted): operating / investing /
financing categories in the P&L, mandatory subtotals (operating profit, profit before financing
and income taxes), management-defined performance measures note, aggregation rules. If the
period is 2027+ or early-adopted, use IFRS 18 layout and present the transition reconciliation.

## US GAAP
Balance sheet (classified), income statement (single- or multi-step), comprehensive income,
stockholders' equity, cash flows (ASC 230), notes. For SEC registrants: Regulation S-X captions
(Rule 5-02 / 5-03), and ASU 2024-03 disaggregation of income statement expenses when effective.
Leases ASC 842 (operating vs finance), revenue ASC 606, credit losses ASC 326 (CECL), income
taxes ASC 740 (incl. ASU 2023-09 rate reconciliation and jurisdictional disclosure).

## Choosing the format quickly
| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Indian listed company, or net worth ≥ ₹250 crore, or its holding/subsidiary/JV/associate | Ind AS, Schedule III Div II (Div III if NBFC) |
| Other Indian company | AS, Schedule III Div I |
| Foreign subsidiary for its local filing | Local GAAP/IFRS as required there |
| Foreign subsidiary for Indian consolidation | Restate to parent's framework (Ind AS) |
| US company / US-listed | US GAAP |
| Most other jurisdictions | IFRS or IFRS-converged local GAAP |
