# 10 — Sector overlays

Apply the overlay for the entity's sector on top of the base framework. Confirm sector-specific
regulator guidance is current.

| Sector | Format / standards | Key disclosures and checks |
|---|---|---|
| **NBFC / HFC** | Schedule III Div III; RBI Master Directions (scale-based regulation) | ECL staging (Stage 1/2/3) and reconciliation to IRACP provisions; impairment reserve; capital adequacy (CRAR); asset-liability maturity buckets; sector exposure; restructured accounts; LCR (where applicable); RBI-mandated schedule to balance sheet |
| **Banks** | Banking Regulation Act Third Schedule (Forms A & B) | Not Schedule III — use RBI formats; Basel III Pillar 3 separately |
| **Insurance** | IRDAI regulations formats (Revenue Account, P&L, BS) | Solvency margin, claims development |
| **Real estate / construction** | Ind AS 115 (point vs over time), Guidance Note on real estate | Inventory (land, WIP, finished units); contract assets/liabilities; borrowing cost capitalisation; RERA escrow balances |
| **Flex / managed office, leasing-heavy (incl. co-working, retail, hospitality)** | Ind AS 116 lessee + lessor (sub-lease classification) | ROU and lease liability roll-forwards; sub-lease: finance vs operating by reference to head-lease ROU; EBITDA pre- and post-Ind AS 116; security deposits at amortised cost (Ind AS 109 discounting); lease modifications and rent concessions; fit-out capex depreciation over shorter of useful life and lease term; centre-level reporting if segment |
| **Manufacturing** | Ind AS 2, 16 | Raw material consumption by class; capacity and production (where relevant); inventory valuation; CWIP ageing; government grants (PLI); excise/customs contingencies |
| **IT / ITES / SaaS** | Ind AS 115, 38 | Unbilled revenue vs contract assets; deferred revenue; capitalised development costs; export receivables FEMA realisation; SEZ/STPI benefits; ESOP charge |
| **GCC / captive service providers** | Ind AS 115 (cost-plus), transfer pricing | Markup basis; TP adjustments; foreign currency receivables from parent; RPT dominance — RPT note and matrix are central |
| **GIFT City IFSC units** | IFSCA regulations; functional currency usually USD | Functional currency determination; presentation currency; IFSCA-specific reporting; tax holiday (s.80LA) disclosure |
| **Pharma / healthcare** | Ind AS 38 (R&D), 115 | R&D capitalisation criteria; sales returns provisions; chargebacks; regulatory contingencies |
| **Retail / D2C / e-commerce** | Ind AS 115 | Principal vs agent; returns and refunds liability; marketplace commissions; inventory shrinkage; loyalty points (deferred revenue) |
| **Power / infrastructure** | Ind AS 115, Appendix D of Ind AS 115 (service concessions) | Regulatory deferral account balances (Ind AS 114); concession intangible vs financial asset |
| **Startups (pre-revenue / early)** | Ind AS or AS per thresholds | Going concern assessment; CCPS/CCD classification (equity vs liability — Ind AS 32 fixed-for-fixed test); ESOP; DPIIT/80-IAC disclosure |
| **Trusts / Section 8** | Schedule III Div I (s.8 companies), ICAI formats for NPOs | Fund accounting; restricted vs unrestricted funds; FCRA receipts |

## Listed company overlay (SEBI LODR) — add regardless of sector
- Segment reporting (Ind AS 108) consistent with quarterly results segments.
- EPS basic and diluted.
- Related party disclosures + Reg 23 matrix (references/05).
- Statement of deviation/variation in utilisation of issue proceeds (Reg 32) and monitoring agency
  reference where IPO/QIP/preferential proceeds exist.
- Credit rating, NCD/debt securities disclosures (Reg 52/54: asset cover, security, key ratios
  — for listed debt).
- Reconciliation of quarterly results to annual audited figures (Q4 = annual − 9 months).
- Dividend distribution policy reference; unclaimed dividend/IEPF transfers.
- Events after the reporting period, including material events disclosed under Reg 30.
- Use publicly available information (annual reports, exchange filings, investor presentations)
  if web access exists to cross-check prior-year comparatives, segment definitions and accounting
  policy wording. Cite the source; never override the user's TB with public numbers.
